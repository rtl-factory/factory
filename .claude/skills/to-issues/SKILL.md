---
name: to-issues
description: Open any GitHub issue, or turn an aligned plan, spec or conversation into labelled issues or an epic. Use before opening any issue (a slice, a bug, a follow-up, an audit log), when work needs to be split, or when triaging an incoming issue.
---

Follow `wiki/bkm/issues.md`: open every issue with its template from its Templates section and its labels from its Labels section, whatever route opens it; split the work into vertical slices that each end in a verifiable state, give each acceptance criteria, "blocked by" links and labels, and make an epic only when the slices cannot reach `main` one by one.
