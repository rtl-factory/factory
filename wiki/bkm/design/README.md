# Design BKMs

| BKM | Use it when |
|---|---|
| [grilling.md](grilling.md) | a plan, decision or idea needs stress-testing before work starts, or someone asks to be grilled |
| [domain-modeling.md](domain-modeling.md) | a term is fuzzy or conflicts with the glossary, or a decision may deserve an ADR |
| [glossary-format.md](glossary-format.md) | writing an entry in a `GLOSSARY.md` |
| [adr-format.md](adr-format.md) | writing an ADR |

A grilling session always runs with domain-modeling alongside it: settled terms go into the glossary and qualifying decisions into ADRs as they are made, not at the end.
