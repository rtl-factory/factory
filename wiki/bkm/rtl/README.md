# RTL

The `rtl` discipline: a project's HAS and each block's MAS ([standard documents](../design/standard-docs.md)) and the design of the blocks they describe.

Shaping blocks and the seams between them: designing deep blocks with narrow interfaces, finding and proposing deepening opportunities, refactoring safely, and explaining how and why a block is built, in [architecture](architecture.md).

Writing and reviewing SystemVerilog RTL: names, formatting, flops, reset, structs, enums and state machines, parameters, packages and assertions, in [RTL coding](coding.md). The RTL author and the reviewer follow the same rules.
